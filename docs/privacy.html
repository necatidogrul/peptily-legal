Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

Peptily has no account. Everything you enter — compounds, doses, injection sites, notes, weights, meals, check-ins and progress photos — is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud backup if you have one enabled. We cannot see it. The one exception is a meal photo you choose to scan (see “Meal scanning”): it is sent for analysis, the result comes back, and the photo is not kept.

Health data

What you log in Peptily is health-related information. It stays in the app's private container on your device. Peptily does not read from or write to Apple Health. Peptily does not share, sell, rent or transfer your health information to anyone, and it is never used for advertising or marketing.

Photos

If you attach progress photos, they are stored inside the app's own container on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Peptily asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to add a photo or scan a meal.

Meal scanning

When you scan a meal, Peptily sends that one photo over an encrypted connection to our scanning service, which passes it to Google’s Gemini model to estimate the food, portion, protein, fiber and calories. The estimate is returned to your device and saved there. The photo is not stored by our service — it is held only for the length of the request and is never written to a database or log — and it is not used for advertising.

To count free scans, each request also carries a random identifier kept in your device’s Keychain and the anonymous RevenueCat identifier used for purchases. Our service stores only these identifiers with a count of scans and whether you have Pro. No name, email, health log or photo is stored with them. Scanning is optional: you can always add a meal by hand instead.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify purchase status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, your purchase receipt, device type and app version — never your protocol data, your name or your email. Apple's App Store handles payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising attribution

If you installed Peptily after tapping an Apple Search Ads advert, Apple provides an attribution token that tells us which campaign led to the install. This is Apple's first-party AdServices framework. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and Peptily does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track you.

Analytics

Peptily contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting service beyond what Apple provides to developers in aggregate. The app does send RevenueCat a few anonymous in-app step counters, attached to the same anonymous identifier used for purchases (for example “setup finished”, “dose step reached and how long it took”, “first dose logged”, “offer screen shown” and which screen it was opened from). They contain no compound names, doses, injection sites, weights, notes or any other health data, and are used only to improve the app.

Your rights

Your log never leaves your device, so you control it completely. The scan counter described under “Meal scanning” holds no personal or health information; email us if you would like it deleted. Deleting the app deletes the data. You can export your log as a PDF at any time from Settings. There is nothing on our side to request, correct or erase; if you would still like written confirmation of that, email us.

Children

Peptily is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
