Where do the numbers in my reminders come from?

From you. Enter your dose and, if you have it, the syringe reading exactly as your prescription or pharmacy label gives them. Peptily stores them as typed and shows them on every reminder. It does not calculate, convert, check or suggest any amount — ask your prescriber or pharmacist if you are unsure what to draw.

Where did the syringe reading on my protocol go?

Protocols set up before version 1.5 need the syringe reading entered once more. Open the protocol and enter the reading from your prescription; your doses and history are unchanged.

How does the vial reminder work?

Set the day you started a vial and after how many days to replace it, using the date on your label or the instructions that came with it. Peptily reminds you three days before.

I did not get a reminder

Check Settings → Notifications → Peptily is allowed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not filtering it. Peptily only sends reminders on dose days and the vial replace-by reminder you set — nothing else.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything; there is no server copy. Export a PDF first from Settings if you want to keep a record.
