Where does the dose in my reminders come from?

From you. Enter your dose exactly as your prescription or pharmacy label gives it. Peptily stores it as typed and shows it on every reminder. Ask your prescriber or pharmacist if you have questions about your dose.

How does the vial reminder work?

Set the day you started a vial and after how many days to replace it, using the date on your label or the instructions that came with it. Peptily reminds you three days before.

I did not get a reminder

Check Settings → Notifications → Peptily is allowed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not filtering it. Peptily only sends reminders on dose days and the vial replace-by reminder you set — nothing else.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything; there is no server copy. Export a PDF first from Settings if you want to keep a record.
